This Privacy Policy describes how Transrail Logistics Limited ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Truck Duniya mobile application ("the App").

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following personal information when you register and use the App:

  • Name: for account identification
  • Email address: for login and account management
  • Phone number: for account verification
  • Vehicle details: for FASTag and fleet management features
  • Payment transaction data: order ID, amount, and transaction status for FASTag recharge (processed via CCAvenue / HDFC payment gateway)
  • Device information and crash logs: collected automatically for app stability (via Firebase)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your Truck Duniya account
  • To process FASTag recharge transactions securely
  • To provide customer support
  • To improve the App's performance and fix issues
  • To send transaction receipts and important account notifications

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • CCAvenue / HDFC Bank: to process payment transactions
  • Firebase (Google LLC): for authentication, database, and crash reporting
  • National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI): for FASTag recharge processing
  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co): to deliver enquiries you send through the website's Contact and Sales Partner forms to our team's email

All third-party services are bound by their own privacy policies and applicable data protection laws.

4. Data Security

All data transmitted between the App and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS. Payment transactions are processed through PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways. We do not store card or UPI credentials on our servers.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. Transaction records are retained for 7 years as required by Indian financial regulations. You may request deletion of your account by contacting us at reachus@truckduniya.co.in.

6. Children's Privacy

Truck Duniya is not intended for users under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To make a request, contact us at reachus@truckduniya.co.in.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes via the App or email.
