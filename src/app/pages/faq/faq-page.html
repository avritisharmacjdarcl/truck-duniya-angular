Frequently asked

What is Truck Duniya?
Truck Duniya is India's one-stop platform for trucking, multiple essential services bundled into a single app for fleet owners, transporters and drivers. It's operated by Transrail Logistics Limited, and every feature is designed around the realities of running actual trucks on Indian highways.
Which services are available right now?
FASTag, Load Opportunities, and GPS Tracking are live. All other services are being progressively rolled out across India. Sign up to be notified when they become available in your region.
Who is Truck Duniya built for?
Built for every trucking business, from single truck operators to large fleet owners. Truck Duniya brings everything you need onto one connected platform, enabling you to operate smarter, reduce costs, and grow with confidence.
How is Truck Duniya different from other apps?
Truck Duniya is more than a trucking app. Operated by Transrail Logistics Limited, it combines essential trucking services on one platform, making it easier for truck owners and fleet operators to save time, reduce costs, and run their business with confidence.
What documents do I need to sign up?
Aadhaar or PAN for identity, RC book for your vehicle, and a working mobile number. That's it to open an account. Additional documents come in only when you activate financing or high-value services.
Do I pay for the app?
Downloading and using the app is free. You only pay for specific services you activate, a FASTag, an insurance policy, a fuel card, a financing product, hardware devices. Every price is shown upfront.
How quickly will my FASTag arrive?
Once your order is confirmed, your NHAI-certified tag ships within 48 hours. In many locations our team delivers and activates it right at your loading point or doorstep.
What documents do I need for FASTag KYC?
Any one government-issued ID, Aadhaar, PAN, RC book, Driving Licence, or GST certificate for fleet owners. Upload both sides; verification is fully digital and takes under two minutes.
My tag isn't beeping at the toll, what do I do?
First, check your wallet balance in the app. If it's fine, make sure the tag is mounted correctly inside the windshield. If it still fails, call our 24×7 helpline and we'll send a replacement to your next stop.
Can I get GST-compliant invoices?
Yes. Every toll, fuel spend and transaction is GST-tagged. Consolidated invoices are generated automatically on the 1st of each month. Download from the app or have them emailed to your accounts team.
How do I recharge my FASTag wallet?
Through UPI, net banking, or credit/debit card. You can also turn on auto top-up, so your wallet refills automatically when it drops below a limit you set.
How does financing on real data work?
Our RBI-regulated lending partners look at your FASTag, GPS and fuel-card history to underwrite. A truck that runs 25 days a month, pays tolls consistently and shows healthy fuel patterns is a great business, and gets a fairer rate than paperwork-only lending.
How do I get support?
Call our 24×7 helpline, email us, or chat inside the app. Most issues are resolved in hours, by a real person who understands your business.
