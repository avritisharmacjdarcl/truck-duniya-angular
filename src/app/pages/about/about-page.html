Who we are

About Transrail Logistics Limited

Truck Duniya is a service brand operated by Transrail Logistics Limited.

Transrail Logistics Limited is an Indian logistics company focused on making fleet management smarter, simpler and more efficient. Through Truck Duniya, we bring digital capabilities within reach of truck owners, transporters and fleet operators through one integrated platform.

Truck Duniya is a comprehensive digital logistics platform for fleet owners of all sizes, from small operators to large fleets, helping them keep their fleets connected, track trucks in real time, simplify toll payments and improve fleet utilisation through better return-load opportunities.

We combine our specialised capabilities with the strength, experience and legacy of CJ Darcl Logistics Limited, our holding company. As part of the CJ Darcl Group, we remain committed to reliability, service excellence and delivering lasting value to our customers.
