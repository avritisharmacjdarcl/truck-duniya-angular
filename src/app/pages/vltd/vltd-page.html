What is VLTD

The government-mandated GPS device for commercial vehicles.

A Vehicle Location Tracking Device (VLTD) is a GPS-enabled tracking unit built to AIS-140 standards. It is mandatory for public service vehicles, national permit trucks, and school buses in many states, and increasingly required for full commercial fleet compliance.

  • Continuous location transmission at fixed intervals
  • Emergency button connected to state control rooms
  • Backup battery for uninterrupted tracking
  • Certified against ARAI / ICAT test protocols
